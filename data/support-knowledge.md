# Customer Support Knowledge Base

## Payment Failed

If a customer's payment fails, ask them to verify that their payment method is active and that the billing information is correct.

The customer can retry the payment after checking their payment method.

If the payment continues to fail after retrying, the issue should be escalated to human support.

## Login Problems

If a customer cannot log in, they should first verify that they are using the correct email address and password.

If they forgot their password, they should use the password reset option on the login page.

If the customer still cannot access the account after resetting the password, escalate the issue to human support.

## Profile Changes

Customers can update their profile information from their account settings.

If a customer cannot edit their profile information, the issue should be escalated to human support.

## Refund Requests

Refund requests must be reviewed by human support.

The AI agent must not promise or approve a refund.

## Account Security

Never ask customers to provide their password, authentication codes, or other sensitive account credentials.

Security-related issues should be escalated to human support.

## Human Escalation

Escalate the request when:

- The knowledge base does not contain enough information.
- The customer reports a security issue.
- The customer requests a refund approval.
- The customer still cannot access their account after troubleshooting.
- The customer requires account-specific action that the AI cannot perform.
